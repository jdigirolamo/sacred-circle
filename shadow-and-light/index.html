What happens

You don’t have to carry it alone.

Nothing here is a surprise

If you’ve never done this before, here is exactly how it goes — start to finish.

  1. Before

    Wear something loose and comfortable. Nothing to prepare, read, or believe in. Come exactly as you are.

  2. Talk

    Ten or fifteen minutes: what brings you, what you would like held, and anything to leave alone — including whether there is any touch at all.

  3. Session

    Fully clothed on a padded table, lights low, quiet music, a blanket if you want one. Hands rest lightly on or just above the body. Sleeping is fine.

  4. Afterward

    We come back slowly, with water and a little time. Some people feel it for a day or two; some feel nothing much. Both are normal.
