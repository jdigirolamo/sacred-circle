## Platform

web

## Users

Primary visitors are adults in Calgary (and virtual clients) looking for gentle, trauma-informed healing or a clear next step into care — especially people new to Reiki who need to feel safe before booking. Secondary audiences include parents exploring daycare that honours kids as they are (Rainbow Roots), and people seeking chart-led self-understanding (Astrology Discovery). Site owners are Jonathan DiGirolamo (build/ops) and Felicia DiGirolamo (Shadow & Light practitioner and creative healer).

## Product Purpose

Sacred Circle is the umbrella brand and front door for nested offerings that share one warm Calgary home. The site orients visitors to the right path under the circle, then sends them deeper — with Shadow & Light Reiki fully bookable today, and Rainbow Roots and Astrology Discovery as lighter intros while those businesses grow. Success means a clear, trustworthy home page that feels like Sacred Circle (not a single clinic), and conversion into Reiki booking or contact when that is what the visitor needs.

## Positioning

One shared ground for separate businesses that keep their own pace and voice — healing, kids who can be themselves, and discovering who you really are — without demanding a worldview or replacing professional healthcare.

## Operating Context

Live on Netlify at https://sacred-circle.netlify.app, source in GitHub `jdigirolamo/sacred-circle` (`main`, publish directory `.`, no build command). ## Product Principles

1. Sacred Circle is the home; nested businesses keep their own pages and pace.
2. Orient first, then deepen — never cram every offering into the hub.
3. Complementary care only; never imply clinical replacement.
4. Prefer confirmed copy and assets; leave gaps explicit rather than inventing.
5. Keep the legacy shadownlight site and Sacred Circle deploy separate unless asked otherwise.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No product-specific WCAG target was set beyond ordinary web practice. Trauma-informed, non-judgmental tone on S&L; touch-friendly booking CTAs; avoid requiring belief systems to engage.
